Before you message anyone

UK law (PECR) treats direct messages on platforms such as Airbnb and LinkedIn as electronic mail. Message individuals and sole traders only if they have given consent to hear from you, and respect every opt-out straight away. Airbnb's Terms do not allow commercial messages without the recipient's consent. You send every message yourself, from your own account, and you are responsible for it.

Business email

You send each one

When does a business email need consent? You can email a limited company or LLP without asking first, as long as you say who you are and give an easy way to opt out. This covers the company's own email addresses, such as those on its own website domain. A personal address such as Gmail belongs to an individual, so email it only if they have agreed. Sole traders and ordinary partnerships count as individuals, so email them only if they have agreed to hear from you. Whoever you write to, stop as soon as they ask. If you write to a named person at a company, data protection law covers their details too.

Token: {company}. Queue adds the company and its finished email to the tracker, where you copy it into your own email. Every email ends with your name, business and reply email, then an opt-out line: “If you would rather not hear from me again, reply "unsubscribe" or email [your reply email] and I will not contact you again.” ReelSieve always adds that ending, so it cannot be left out by mistake. When you queue, your name, business name and reply email are saved to your account so they are filled in next time. Deleting your account deletes them.
