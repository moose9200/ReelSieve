Listing content and your videos

When you paste a listing link, our servers fetch the public listing page: its photos, title, host first name and public guest reviews. We use them only to build that video. The working files are deleted from our servers as soon as the job ends. Videos show the text, star rating and month of a guest review, but not the reviewer's name. Finished videos go to your own Google Drive and stay there under your control. We fetch these public pages logged out and at a limited rate. When Airbnb blocks us we pause at once, and if it blocks us again we stop until a person has checked. A host can ask us to stop making reels of their listing with the privacy request form (choose "Remove my listing from ReelSieve").

Listing photos shown inside ReelSieve are loaded through our own server, so Airbnb does not receive your IP address when you look at them. Our fonts are served from our own server too.

Cookies

We set two cookies, both strictly necessary, so we do not ask for consent:

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in. It lasts 12 hours, or 30 days if you choose to stay signed in.
  • Security cookie: protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It lasts 30 days.

If you use the light/dark switch, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage (under the name lr-theme). It stays on your device and is never sent to us. If you do not want it stored, do not use the switch, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data.

Who we share data with

  • Railway hosts the website, the video workers and the database, in the United States. Railway processes data only on our instructions under its data processing agreement.
  • Google receives your finished videos when we save them to your Drive. Google acts as an independent controller under its own terms.
  • Higgsfield receives listing photos only if you switch on AI camera motion for a reel. It uses them to generate the video clips. Higgsfield acts as its own controller, and its privacy policy says it may use inputs to improve its AI models. Leave AI camera motion off if you do not want your photos shared.
  • Photon (a map search service run by komoot) receives the place names you type into the listing search. The request comes from our server, so it never receives your IP address.
  • Stripe takes card payments when card checkout is switched on. Stripe receives your email and payment details directly and acts as its own controller for payment data. Card details never reach our servers.

We may also share data where the law requires it, for example with a tax authority.

Braivex is based in India, and our hosting is in the United States. When we pass personal data from the UK or the EU to Railway or Stripe, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in their data processing agreements, and on their certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension. Google transfers are covered by Google's controller terms. Ask us for a copy of the safeguards through the privacy request form.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send it to you in a machine-readable form. You can also object to our use of it where we rely on legitimate interests.

  • Download your data: Account, then "Download my data".
  • Delete your account: Account, then "Delete my account". We delete or anonymise your data at once. We keep paid-order records for the period above, and your videos stay in your own Google Drive.
  • Anything else: use the privacy request form. The form acknowledges your request straight away with a reference. We reply within one month. If a request is complex we may extend that by up to two months and will tell you why within the first month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Complaints

Please complain to us first, using the privacy request form. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, helpline 0303 123 1113. In the EU you can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

If you are an Airbnb host or guest

ReelSieve users can make videos from public Airbnb listings, and can track hosts they choose to contact. So some of your details may pass through ReelSieve even if you have never used it.

  • What we get: for hosts, first name, public Airbnb profile link, listing link, listing photos and title. For guests, the text, rating, month and first name shown on a public review.
  • Where it comes from: public Airbnb listing and profile pages.
  • Why: to make videos our users request, and to let users keep a record of hosts they message themselves. We rely on legitimate interests.
  • How long: listing content is deleted when the video job ends. Host names and review details are removed from job records after 30 days. Outreach records are deleted 12 months after they last changed. Listings a host asks us to remove are kept on our block list (number, request reference and date) until the host asks us to lift it. Guest names never appear in videos.
  • Stop reels of your listing: use the privacy request form, choose "Remove my listing from ReelSieve" and give the listing link. We normally block it at once, while we check your request: no ReelSieve user can make a reel of it or find it through Find a listing, co-host search or Outreach.
  • Your right to object: you can object at any time, and we will stop. Use the privacy request form and choose "Object to outreach", giving your Airbnb profile link. We add you to a permanent do-not-contact list that stores only a keyed hash, and ReelSieve will no longer show you to any user as someone to contact.

ReelSieve never sends messages. Users who contact hosts do so from their own accounts and are responsible for doing so lawfully.

Google user data

ReelSieve asks Google only for the drive.file permission. We use it to create the finished video files in your Google Drive and to change their sharing when you ask us to. We cannot see any other files in your Drive. We store an encrypted access token until you disconnect or delete your account. We do not use Google data for advertising or to train AI models, and we do not sell or transfer it except as needed to provide the service. ReelSieve's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from your Account page, and remove ReelSieve's access in your Google Account settings.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes. Google tokens are encrypted. The site is served only over HTTPS, every form is protected against request forgery, and each customer can reach only their own data. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

Children

ReelSieve is for businesses and adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update this page when our use of data changes, and change the date at the top.
