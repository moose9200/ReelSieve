Listing content and your videos

When you paste a listing link, our servers fetch the public listing page: its photos, title, town, rating, host first name and Airbnb user number, and public guest reviews. We use them only to build that video. The photos and review text are deleted from our servers when the job ends. Your reel history keeps the listing's title, town, rating and link, and the link to its main photo that the reel's page shows. The host's name and user number are removed after 30 days. Videos show the text, star rating and month of a guest review, but not the reviewer's name. Finished videos go to your own Google Drive and stay there under your control. We fetch these public pages logged out and at a limited rate, shared by all of ReelSieve. When Airbnb blocks us we pause all fetching at once. If it blocks us again within a day of a pause, or refuses a page for legal reasons, we stop until a person has checked. A host can ask us to stop making reels of their listing with the privacy request form (choose "Remove my listing from ReelSieve").

When you make a reel from your own photos instead, nothing is fetched from Airbnb. We handle the photos and the details you type as described in the table above. Only the words and star rating of a guest quote appear in the video, never a name.

Listing photos shown inside ReelSieve are loaded through our own server, so Airbnb does not receive your IP address when you look at them. Our fonts are served from our own server too.

Cookies

We set two cookies, both strictly necessary, so we do not ask for consent:

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in. It is deleted when you close your browser and stops working after 12 hours at most, or lasts 30 days if you choose to stay signed in.
  • Security cookie: protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It lasts 30 days.

If you use the light/dark switch, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage (under the name lr-theme). It stays on your device and is never sent to us. If you do not want it stored, do not use the switch, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data.

If you are an Airbnb host or guest

ReelSieve users can make videos from public Airbnb listings, and can track hosts they choose to contact. So some of your details may pass through ReelSieve even if you have never used it.

  • What we get: for hosts, first name, Airbnb user number and public profile link, listing link, listing photos and title, how many listings they have and whether they are a Superhost. For guests, the text, rating, month and first name shown on a public review.
  • Where it comes from: public Airbnb listing, search and Co-Host Network pages. We link to hosts' public profile pages but do not fetch them.
  • Why: to make videos our users request, and to let users keep a record of hosts they message themselves. We rely on legitimate interests.
  • How long: listing photos and review text are deleted when the video job ends. The listing's title, town, rating and link stay in the customer's reel history. Host names and user numbers, and the review details and message drafted for the host, are removed from job records after 30 days. Outreach records are deleted 12 months after they last changed. Listings a host asks us to remove are kept on our block list (number, request reference and date): provisionally for one month while we check the request, then, once confirmed, until the host asks us to lift it. Guest names never appear in videos.
  • Stop reels of your listing: use the privacy request form, choose "Remove my listing from ReelSieve" and give the listing link. We normally block it at once, while we check your request: no ReelSieve user can make a reel of it or find it through Find a listing, co-host search or Outreach.
  • Your right to object: you can object at any time, and we will stop. Use the privacy request form and choose "Object to outreach", giving your Airbnb profile link. We add you to a permanent do-not-contact list at once. It stores only a keyed hash and your request's reference, and ReelSieve will no longer show you to any user as someone to contact, including on the page of a reel made from your listing. Because anyone can send the form, we check requests, and undo an entry only if someone else sent it in your name.

ReelSieve never sends messages. Users who contact hosts do so from their own accounts and are responsible for doing so lawfully.

If a customer quotes your review: a customer who makes a reel from their own photos can type in up to three quotes from reviews guests left for their property. They must confirm the quotes are real and word for word. We keep only the words and the star rating, never a name. The reel shows the first quote. We remove the quotes from job records 30 days after the job ends. You can object through the privacy request form.

If your company is on the Companies House register

ReelSieve users can search UK property companies and choose which ones to email about their services. So your company's register details may be shown to ReelSieve users even if you have never used ReelSieve. Many small companies carry their owner's name, so these details can sometimes identify a person.

  • What we hold: for active UK companies (limited, unlimited and community interest companies) and LLPs whose business is holiday and short-stay accommodation or managing property for others, the company name and number, post town, the first half of the registered office postcode (for example BH1) and business category (SIC code). We do not hold officer or shareholder details, the street address, the full postcode or any email address.
  • Where it comes from: the Companies House Free Company Data Product, a monthly snapshot of the public register.
  • Why: to let ReelSieve users find UK property businesses they may want to offer their services to. We rely on legitimate interests. Users find any email address themselves, and every email ReelSieve drafts for them says who it is from and how to opt out.
  • How long: we replace the whole list with each monthly snapshot, so a company leaves ReelSieve when it leaves the snapshot, for example when it is dissolved. A user's own tracker records are deleted 12 months after they last changed.
  • Your right to object: use the privacy request form, choose "Object to outreach" and give your company number. We add the company to a permanent do-not-contact list at once. It stores only a keyed hash and your request's reference, and ReelSieve will no longer show the company to any user. Because anyone can send the form, we check requests, and undo an entry only if someone else sent it in the company's name. You can also reply to any ReelSieve user who emails you and ask them to stop.

ReelSieve never sends these emails. Users send each one from their own email account and are responsible for doing so lawfully.

Google user data

ReelSieve asks Google for the drive.file permission, which reaches only files ReelSieve creates, plus your Google account's email address and ID to show which account is connected. We use Drive to save your finished videos in your Google Drive, to change their sharing when you ask us to, and to play or download a reel inside ReelSieve: the video passes through our server on its way to you and is not stored. If you make a reel from your own photos, we also save the photos in an Inputs folder there, our video worker reads them back to make the reel, and it deletes them from your Drive when you ask. We cannot see any other files in your Drive. We store encrypted Google access and refresh tokens until you disconnect or delete your account. We do not use Google data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models ourselves. We pass it on only as needed to provide the service, and never to an AI provider: photos read back from your Drive are not sent to Higgsfield. ReelSieve's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from your Account page, and remove ReelSieve's access in your Google Account settings.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes. Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted. The site is served only over HTTPS, every form is protected against request forgery, and each customer can reach only their own data. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.
